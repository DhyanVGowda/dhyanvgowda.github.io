Computer and Information Research Scientist (Software Engineering and Applied AI) - MG Health Tech

McKinney, Texas | February 2026 - Present

Most of my work at MG Health Tech is in remote patient monitoring and home sleep testing. On VitalWeave, I co-developed the integration of Bluetooth blood pressure and ECG devices into the mobile app, so readings reach the patient's physician.


The home sleep testing work starts with the capture engine I'm developing, which records overnight data from a Bluetooth pulse oximeter. The recordings go to the server, which gets them ready for sleep scoring and connects to the MySleepReport physician portal.


The AI voice agent I co-built answers calls for multiple clinics, and its HIPAA and TCPA safeguards were my responsibility.


Other projects include taking the AroCord health record summarizer to production, designing the MySQL database shared by VitalWeave and MySleepReport, and setting up the AWS backend for all three products. Separately, I wrote the technical plan for hospital private 5G edge AI and 6G contactless sensing with a global telecom vendor.


Software Development Intern - MG Health Tech

Remote | June 2025 - August 2025

A patent-pending system I built summarizes a patient's medical documents in under a minute, cutting clinic intake by up to 15 minutes. A check I added compares each summary against its source documents and removes anything unsupported. The internship also included deploying MedGemma for medical image analysis and building a RAG assistant that answers doctors' questions from patient records.

Teaching Assistant, Generative AI and Software Project Management - Illinois Institute of Technology

Chicago, Illinois | January 2025 - December 2025 (two semesters)

Over two semesters as a TA, I worked with more than 100 graduate students each semester as they built generative AI applications and learned to manage software projects.


The labs I developed covered AutoGen, LangGraph, and LlamaIndex. I also ran live demos of full-stack projects, including React/Flask microservices, Google Cloud deployments, and TensorFlow LSTM models.


During one-on-one office hours, students brought questions on Python, JavaScript, AI agent architectures, and Agile/Scrum. I also mentored students on RAG pipelines, vector embeddings, Elasticsearch, Docker, agentic AI, and the OpenAI, Replicate, and Ollama APIs.

Software Development Intern - Torus Solutions

Mysuru, India | February 2023 - November 2023

At Torus I worked on a food recycling platform that gets surplus food to underprivileged communities. Donors offer food, people in need send a request, and each request is assigned to an NGO in their area, which accepts it and delivers the food. Email updates keep both sides informed.


The backend was Java Spring Boot with MySQL, the frontend was React, and we worked in Agile sprints. My part included sign-in for the different user roles, the NGO and donor matching, and MySQL performance tuning.
